About us

Why We Started This

"Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not."

Millions of talented students never get access to mentorship, practical education, networking, or investment opportunities because of financial and social barriers.

Beyond Circumstances was created to bridge that gap and help young people transform their ideas into real opportunities.

At Beyond Circumstances, we want to help kids who have passionate ideas but can't act on them because of factors beyond their control.
