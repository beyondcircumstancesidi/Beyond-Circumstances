    Phase 2

    Build

    Turn your idea into something real, one step at a time. You can always go back and edit an earlier step — you just can't skip ahead until the current one is submitted for mentor review.

    1Research
    2Plan
    3Pitch
    AI Mentor

    Ask a question, anytime.

    Your mentor won't just hand you answers — it'll help you think it through. Ask about whatever step you're on.

    Confirm the problem is real.

    Don't guess. Find evidence this problem is real and worth solving — read what's already out there, or go talk directly to people who deal with it. Either counts, but you need real evidence. Log what you find below.

    Write your project or business plan.

    Turn your idea into an actual plan. Cover what the problem is, who it affects, what you're proposing, and why it could realistically work — in your own words.

    Get one person to actually read your plan and react to it. Type what they said below.

    Practice your pitch.

    An elevator pitch says what the problem is, who has it, what you're building, and why it matters — out loud, in under a minute. Work out what you want to say, then record yourself actually saying it.
